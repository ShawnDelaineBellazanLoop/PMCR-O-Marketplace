# Pattern Learner Domain Skill

Extracts and crystallizes recurring patterns across PMCR-O cycles into reusable knowledge.

## Role

The Pattern Learner identifies recurring patterns across sessions and cycles, and crystallizes them into reusable knowledge — including new EarnedConstraints.

## Key Design Rules

1. **Pattern extraction** — identify recurring patterns across sessions and cycles.
2. **Constraint crystallization** — turn patterns into first-person, specific, observable constraint statements.
3. **Reusable knowledge** — patterns become reusable knowledge for future cycles.

## Guardrails

1. Every pattern names its source cycles and the evidence for it.
2. Constraints are always written in first-person, specific, observable form.
3. "No action needed" is a valid decision — not every input requires intervention.

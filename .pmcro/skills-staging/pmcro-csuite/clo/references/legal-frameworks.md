# Legal Frameworks Reference — CLO Domain

Common contract risks, regulatory domains, and risk assessment methodology.

## Contract Risk Categories

| Risk | Red-Flag Indicators |
|---|---|
| Unlimited Liability | No cap, uncapped indemnity, no limitation of liability clause |
| IP Assignment | "All IP created" without scope, work-for-hire without exceptions |
| Auto-Renewal | No notice period, no opt-out window, evergreen clauses |
| Termination | One-sided termination rights, excessive notice periods |
| Governing Law | Unfavorable jurisdiction, no venue specification |
| Data/Privacy | Vague data handling, no DPA reference, no breach notification terms |
| Payment Terms | Net-90+, no late payment remedies, one-sided fee adjustment |

## Risk Scoring

Each finding gets a severity:

| Level | Criteria | Action |
|---|---|---|
| LOW | Minor ambiguity, no material exposure | Note, no negotiation required |
| MEDIUM | Could create exposure in specific scenarios | Flag, recommend clarification |
| HIGH | Material risk to company if not addressed | Require revision before signing |
| CRITICAL | Existential risk (unlimited liability, IP loss) | Block — do not sign without resolution |

## Regulatory Domains

Common regulations this domain may encounter:

- **GDPR** (EU data protection): consent, right to erasure, data processing agreements
- **CCPA** (California privacy): disclosure, opt-out, data sale restrictions
- **SOC 2** (Service Organization Controls): security, availability, confidentiality
- **HIPAA** (Healthcare data): PHI handling, BAAs, minimum necessary standard
- **DMCA** (Copyright): takedown procedures, safe harbor provisions

This domain identifies applicable regulations. It does not provide legal advice.

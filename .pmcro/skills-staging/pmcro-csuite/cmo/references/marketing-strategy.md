# Marketing Strategy Reference — CMO Domain

Campaign design, content frameworks, and performance measurement.

## Content Quality Dimensions

Every piece of content is evaluated on:

| Dimension | Score (1-5) | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | Is the message immediately understandable? |
| Brand Voice | Does it match the defined voice? |
| SEO | Keywords, meta, structure, readability |
| CTA Strength | Is the call to action specific and compelling? |
| Audience Fit | Does it address the target persona's need? |

Target: 4+ on all dimensions before publishing.

## Campaign Measurement

### Leading Indicators (during campaign)
- Impressions, clicks, CTR
- Engagement rate (likes, shares, comments)
- Site traffic from campaign sources

### Lagging Indicators (post-campaign)
- Conversion rate (lead, trial, purchase)
- Cost per acquisition (CPA)
- Revenue attributed
- ROI = (Attributed Revenue - Campaign Cost) / Campaign Cost

## Brand Voice Consistency

Brand voice violations are flagged when content:
- Uses a different tone than defined (casual vs. formal vs. technical)
- Addresses a different audience than intended
- Uses terminology inconsistent with brand guidelines
- Makes claims without substantiation (→ CLO risk)

## SEO Framework

On-page SEO checklist:
1. Target keyword in title, H1, first paragraph, and meta description
2. Readability: Flesch-Kincaid grade level appropriate for audience
3. Internal links to 2+ related pages
4. Meta description under 160 characters
5. Image alt text with descriptive keywords

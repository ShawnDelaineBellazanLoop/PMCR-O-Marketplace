# People Operations Reference — CHRO Domain

Hiring frameworks, performance management, and training design.

## Job Description Structure

Every JD includes:

```markdown
# [Role Title]

## Team & Reporting
- Team: [team name]
- Reports to: [manager title]
- Manages: [direct reports, if any]

## Role Summary
[2-3 sentences on what this role does and why it exists]

## Responsibilities
- [Outcome-based responsibility, not task list]
- ...

## Requirements
### Must-Have
- [Non-negotiable requirements]

### Nice-to-Have
- [Would be great but not required]

## Success Measures (First 90 Days)
- 30 days: [orientation + first deliverable]
- 60 days: [independent contribution]
- 90 days: [owning an outcome]
```

## Performance Review Framework

Reviews follow the SBI model:

- **Situation**: when and where
- **Behavior**: what the person did (observable, not interpreted)
- **Impact**: the effect of that behavior on the team/project/company

Ratings use:
- **Exceeds**: consistently delivers above expectations; cited with 2+ examples
- **Meets**: delivers as expected; the standard
- **Developing**: working toward expectations; specific growth plan attached
- **Below**: not meeting expectations; performance improvement plan required

## Training Design

Every training module states:

1. **Learning objective**: "After this training, the learner will be able to..."
2. **Format**: self-paced, live session, workshop, documentation
3. **Duration**: estimated time to complete
4. **Assessment**: how competence is verified (quiz, exercise, observation)
5. **Prerequisites**: what the learner must know before starting
